# Door color review

Generated by `scripts/door-color-review.ts` from the 123 styles in `shared/fixtures/doorStyleSideCatalog.json`.

No color is snapped to the brand list. `brandSnapAvoided` is the old nearest swatch and is not painted.

## How concurrence works

After precedence picks a hex, an independent estimator (the color name, or the DL door-photo solid when the name is silent) compares it. They agree only when CIEDE2000 is at most 10, paint versus stain matches, and the hue family matches. A disagreement keeps the pre-209 color and sets `needsColorReview`. A hand-set render hex is kept and still flagged. One opinion and no second method is applied as chosen.
